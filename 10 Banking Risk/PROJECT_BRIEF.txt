BANKING RISK ANALYST — PROJECT BRIEF

Scenario:
You are a Data Analyst in the Risk Analytics team of a retail bank. Management wants to understand credit risk, repayment behavior, transaction risk, fraud exposure and portfolio concentration.

Deliverables:
1. Data-quality assessment
2. Clean analytical tables
3. SQL analysis
4. Python/Pandas analysis
5. Power BI/Tableau dashboard
6. Answers to all 66 questions
7. Executive summary with business recommendations

Professional expectation:
Do not simply calculate numbers. State the business question, define the metric, validate the data, calculate the result, explain the insight, and recommend an action.
